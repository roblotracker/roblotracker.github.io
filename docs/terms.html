The service

RobloTracker is an independent application that observes supported public Roblox profile changes and can deliver alerts in Discord. These terms apply to the RobloTracker bot and this website. By installing or using RobloTracker, you agree to follow the rules below and the applicable rules of Discord and Roblox.

RobloTracker is independently operated and has no affiliation with or endorsement from Roblox or Discord. Availability and visibility can differ by profile, server, location, endpoint, and over time.

Who may install and use RobloTracker

You may install RobloTracker only in a Discord server where you have permission to manage applications. You must meet the age and eligibility rules that apply to your Discord and Roblox accounts. If you install RobloTracker for a community or organization, obtain the necessary permission and explain to other server managers how the tracker channels are configured.

Only add Roblox accounts when you have an appropriate reason and the authority required by applicable rules. If you’re unsure whether monitoring is welcome, get permission first. Pay particular attention to accounts that may belong to children.

Acceptable use

Use RobloTracker to follow public activity in a way that respects the privacy and safety of the people involved. You may not use RobloTracker to:

  • Harass, stalk, threaten, target, or impersonate someone, or organize harmful activity around someone’s Roblox presence.
  • Attempt to bypass privacy, access, age, or safety controls, or obtain someone’s private activity or credentials.
  • Publish or redistribute tracker data in a way that exposes people to harm or violates applicable rules.
  • Spam alerts or recipients, interfere with RobloTracker’s operation, evade usage limits, or attempt unauthorized access to its systems.
  • Use RobloTracker data as proof for discipline, safety decisions, identity verification, or another high-stakes decision.

Report abuse or security concerns through a private ticket in RobloTracker Support.

Server permissions and alert controls

The Discord authorization screen lists the permissions RobloTracker requests. RobloTracker uses permissions such as Manage Channels and Manage Roles for tracker setup and access, and messaging permissions to deliver and manage alerts. Server operators control which accounts are tracked, who can receive alerts, and the ultimate visibility of channels in their server.

Review the tracker channel after setup and when roles or membership change. Use /settings and the /alerts commands to manage delivery, or use /untrack-account to stop a tracker. Removing RobloTracker from the server stops its access through that installation. It does not erase every copy of earlier Discord messages.

Alert timing and accuracy

RobloTracker checks public API responses on a schedule. The first observation establishes a baseline. Later alerts may be delayed, skipped, or incomplete because of rate limits, server permissions, upstream outages, or information that changes between polls. Game information, named friends, and session duration can be incomplete or estimated.

Use the application as an informational tool. Verify anything important with the relevant platform before acting. RobloTracker does not promise uninterrupted operation, complete coverage, or a fixed delivery time.

Public usage totals

The website may show totals for active tracked accounts and Discord installations. The figures are cached and can lag behind recent setup, removal, or availability changes. They are informational and do not establish any service level or guarantee that a particular tracker is working. See how these totals are handled or use /status to check your server.

Privacy and removing a profile

Read the Privacy Policy for information on collection, storage, visibility, and retention. An account holder can ask RobloTracker to review removal after verifying control with /privacy remove and /privacy verify. If the automated workflow is unavailable, the support team can help with the next step.

Do not share passwords, Roblox session cookies, Discord tokens, or database credentials when using the bot or contacting support.

Changes, restrictions, and removal

Set up trackers responsibly

Before adding a profile, decide why your server needs the tracker and which alerts are necessary. A server that only needs in-game notifications can leave unrelated signals off. Limit recipients to members who need the alerts, and remove access when someone leaves a team or no longer needs it. Review who can see a channel whenever server roles or channel permissions change.

When inviting RobloTracker, check the application name and read Discord’s authorization screen. If a command reports missing permissions, restore only the access needed for the feature or ask a server administrator to review the issue. Avoid granting broad roles solely to work around a configuration error.

Sharing alerts outside your server

Roblox information can be public while a Discord alert about it is still sensitive. Keep alerts and tracker history within the audience that needs them. Before sharing a screenshot or exporting details, consider whether it exposes someone’s activity, usernames, or relationships to people who would otherwise have no reason to see them.

Do not use alert history to build a dossier, compile public lists of monitored people without authorization, or direct unwanted messages at someone. These rules apply to copied and forwarded alerts as well as messages read directly in Discord.

What Roblox and Discord control

Roblox determines which profile fields are publicly available and whether a game or friend list can be shown. A signed-in Roblox session can provide a different view. Changes to a platform can affect coverage, behavior, or the commands available in a given server. RobloTracker respects the responses and access those services provide.

Discord controls availability of the authorization window, delivery of messages, eligibility to install apps, and access through each server’s permissions. A working tracker and an accessible channel do not guarantee a device notification. A recipient’s settings, channel mute, or the platform’s delivery rules can affect whether a notification shows.

Handling misuse and outages

If a tracker is being used against someone, pause or remove the tracker if you have permission, then use a private ticket to report what happened. Include a link to a RobloTracker message, an affected Roblox profile ID, and enough context for staff to understand the issue. Keep the material out of public channels when it could expose private information.

The team may ask for more context, restrict a tracker or installation, or direct an issue to the platform that controls the relevant feature. The team cannot restore someone’s Roblox or Discord account or undo actions by a server moderator. Use the platform’s reporting tools when the problem concerns that platform’s account or safety rules.

Stopping tracking and leaving a server

Use /tracker pause when you want to stop checks temporarily, or /untrack-account when you want to remove the server’s tracker. Server alert delivery can also be disabled separately. Review the relevant confirmation before making a change, especially if the command offers to remove a tracker channel.

If you are the owner of a Roblox profile and want RobloTracker to stop tracking it across installations, follow the verified removal process. You can still contact the team even if you have never added the bot. Leaving a Discord server does not by itself start a Roblox profile-removal request.

Contact the RobloTracker team

Join RobloTracker Support and open a ticket in #ticket-system for a bug, suggestion, terms question, or report of misuse. Check #bot-downstatus if you’re reporting an outage.
